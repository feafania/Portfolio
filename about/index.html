About Me

I'm Tatsiana Kashko, a Junior Web Developer who enjoys building modern, accessible and responsive web applications with clean, maintainable code.

Who I Am

I enjoy solving problems through code and turning ideas into practical, user-friendly digital experiences. What attracts me most to software development is the balance between creativity and logical thinking.

I focus on writing readable, maintainable code and continuously improving my skills through personal projects and learning modern web technologies.

My Journey

My journey in software development started with business application development, where I learned to analyse requirements, solve complex problems and create reliable software solutions. That experience taught me to approach development with accuracy, structure and attention to detail.

As technology evolved, I decided to focus on modern web development. Since then, I've worked extensively with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js and related technologies, building responsive and maintainable web applications while continuously developing my skills.

A Few Facts About Me

Analytical Thinking
